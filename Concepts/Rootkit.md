# Rootkit

A type of [[Malware]] designed to gain and hide privileged ("root"/Administrator-level) access on a system, actively concealing its own presence — and often that of other malware — from the OS, antivirus software, and the user. Can hide inside files using tricks like [[ADS]], or embed itself at the kernel level, making it one of the hardest malware types to detect and remove.
