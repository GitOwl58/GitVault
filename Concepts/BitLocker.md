# BitLocker

A Windows data-protection feature that encrypts an entire drive, guarding against data theft or exposure from a lost, stolen, or improperly decommissioned device. Offers its strongest protection when paired with a [[TPM]] (v1.2+), which also helps verify the system wasn't tampered with while offline.
