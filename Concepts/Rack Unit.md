# Rack Unit

Abbreviated **U** (sometimes RU), the standard unit for measuring the height of rack-mounted equipment and rack/cabinet frames. **1U = 1.75 inches (4.445 cm)**. A device's height is described in U (a "1U server", a "4U NAS chassis"), and a rack's total capacity is described the same way, so a "12U rack" has 12U of usable mounting height.

A full-size datacenter rack is **42U**, far more than any home setup needs. Home and small-office setups commonly use small wall-mount or short freestanding units, often in the 6U-12U range, enough for a router, switch, patch panel, a compute host or two, and a small **[[UPS]]**, without dominating a room.

When shopping for rack-mount gear, it's worth checking not just that a device fits the rack's height in U, but also its **depth**, network gear (routers, switches, patch panels) is typically shallower and produces less heat than server-class gear, so a rack or cabinet sized for network equipment may not have the depth or airflow a server needs.
