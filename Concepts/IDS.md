# IDS (Intrusion Detection System)

Monitors network traffic for suspicious patterns and raises alerts, but unlike a [[Firewall]] doesn't block anything itself, it's passive, detection and logging rather than prevention. A loud, fast network scan is far more likely to trigger one than a slow, spread-out one, which is why tools like [[Nmap]] offer timing controls (see **[[Nmap Timing Templates]]**) for scans meant to stay under the radar.
