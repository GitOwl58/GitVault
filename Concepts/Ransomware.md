# Ransomware

A type of [[Malware]] that encrypts a victim's files (or locks them out of their system) and demands payment — usually in cryptocurrency — in exchange for the decryption key. Often delivered via a [[Trojan]] or phishing attachment, and increasingly paired with a "double extortion" threat to also leak stolen data if the ransom isn't paid.
