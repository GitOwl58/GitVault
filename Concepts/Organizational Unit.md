# Organizational Unit (OU)

A container within [[Active directory]] used to group and organize accounts and computers, mainly so [[Group Policy Object|Group Policy Objects]] can be applied to a whole set of them at once. Distinct from a [[Security Groups|Security Group]], which exists to grant permissions rather than apply policy.
