# sed

The stream editor: reads input line by line and applies a script of edits, most commonly substitution: `sed 's/old/new/g'` replaces every ("g" = global) match of `old` with `new` per line, using [[Regular Expressions]] for the pattern. Chains naturally into pipelines after [[grep]] or other filters.
