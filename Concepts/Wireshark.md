# Wireshark

Open-source, cross-platform network packet analyser: captures live traffic or opens existing capture files (`.pcap`/`.pcapng`) and breaks each packet down layer by layer against the [[OSI Model]]. Purely a read/display tool, not an IDS: it doesn't block or modify traffic, so spotting anomalies depends on the analyst using its filtering, colouring, and stream-following features.
