# NAT (Network Address Translation)

Lets multiple devices on a private network share one public [[IP address]] to reach the Internet — a router rewrites the source address of outgoing packets to its own public IP (and back again for replies). What lets a device on a private range actually get out to the Internet despite not being publicly routable.
