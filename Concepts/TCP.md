# TCP (Transmission Control Protocol)

Reliable, connection-based transport protocol. Reserves a constant connection for the duration of transmission and uses error checking to guarantee data sent in small chunks is received and reassembled in order.

Used for: file sharing, internet browsing, email — anything requiring accurate, complete data.
Operates in Layer 4 of the [[OSI Model]]
