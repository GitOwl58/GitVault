# Internet

One giant network made of many smaller (private) networks joined together by public networks. First iteration: ARPANET (US Defence Dept, late 1960s). The World Wide Web (WWW) was invented in 1989 by Tim Berners-Lee, turning the Internet into a repository for storing/sharing information.
