# Networking

The practice of connecting devices so they can share resources and exchange data — from two computers on a home LAN up to the global [[Internet]]. Devices are identified by an [[IP address]] and a [[MAC address]], moved between networks by [[Router|Routers]] and [[Switch|Switches]], and communicate using layered protocols described by the [[OSI Model]].
