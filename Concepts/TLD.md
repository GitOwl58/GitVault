#type/concept #domain/networking

The last segment of a domain name, following the final dot (e.g. .com in tryhackme.com). Indicates the domain's category or origin — generic (.com, .org, .net), country-code (.fr, .uk), or sponsored/restricted (.gov, .edu). Managed by IANA, delegated to registries who oversee registration within each TLD.
Full TLDs list https://data.iana.org/TLD/tlds-alpha-by-domain.txt
