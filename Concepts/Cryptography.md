### Cryptography

 Cryptography uses mathematical algorithm combined with public and private keys so that only authorized individuals can read specific data.
Cryptography is one of the most critical tools in a defender's arsenal. It protects confidentiality and [[Integrity]], and it's the backbone of almost every secure system used online.
