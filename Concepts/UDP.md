# UDP (User Datagram Protocol)

Fast, connectionless transport protocol — no error checking, no reliability, no synchronization guarantee. Sends data whether or not it's received.

Used for: small discovery packets ([[ARP]], [[DHCP]]) and large files where minor loss is acceptable, e.g. video streaming (pixelation instead of failure).
