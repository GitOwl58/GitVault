# Media Servers

The container that actually streams your library (movies, TV, music) to a phone, TV, or browser. Almost every homelab's media setup starts here, then grows outward into the **[[Media Management (Arr Stack)]]** to automate acquiring and organizing that content.

### Options
- **[[Plex]]**: the most polished apps and easiest sharing, but remote streaming now requires a paid plan.
- **[[Jellyfin]]**: fully open-source and free including remote streaming, the default recommendation for cost-conscious self-hosters as of 2026.
- **Emby**: sits between the two, a freemium model with some features (hardware transcoding, some clients) behind a paid "Emby Premiere" tier, less commonly chosen for a fresh homelab today than either Plex or Jellyfin.
