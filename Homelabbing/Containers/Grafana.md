# Grafana

A dashboarding tool that turns raw metrics, most commonly from **[[Prometheus]]**, into readable graphs and panels. Ships with a large library of community-made dashboards for common exporters (Node Exporter, Docker, a NAS's own metrics), so getting a useful first dashboard running is often a matter of importing one rather than building from scratch.

Also supports alerting (a panel can trigger a notification when a metric crosses a threshold, disk nearly full, a service down), turning passive dashboards into an active early-warning system.
