# Miscellaneous

The self-hosted apps that don't fit neatly into another category but show up often enough in homelabs to be worth listing.

### Options
- **[[Grocy]]**: household and grocery management, tracks pantry stock, shopping lists, chores, and expiry dates.
- **[[Minecraft Server]]**: a genuinely common first "useful" container for a lot of people, easy to justify to family/friends, and a good low-stakes way to practice port forwarding, backups, and resource limits.
