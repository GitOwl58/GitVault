# Uptime Kuma

A simple, self-hosted uptime monitor: define a list of things to check (a URL, a port, a ping target, a DNS record) and an interval, and it shows a clean status page plus history of when each one was up or down. Supports a wide range of notification channels (email, Discord, Telegram, webhooks, and many others) for alerting the moment something goes offline.

Much lower setup effort than a full **[[Prometheus]]** + **[[Grafana]]** stack, the right starting point for "just tell me when something breaks" before reaching for deeper metrics and dashboards.
