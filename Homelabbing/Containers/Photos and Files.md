# Photos and Files

Replacing Google Photos/iCloud/Dropbox with something that lives on your own **[[NAS]]** instead of someone else's cloud, a common "graduation" project once the core homelab is stable.

### Options
- **[[Immich]]**: a self-hosted Google Photos alternative, automatic mobile backup, face recognition, timeline view, has become the clear default in this space by 2026 thanks to fast development and genuinely good mobile apps.
- **[[Nextcloud]]**: broader than just photos, a full file-sync-and-share platform (think self-hosted Dropbox/Google Drive) with photo handling as one of many apps on top, worth it when general file sync and collaboration matter as much as photo backup specifically.
