# Network-wide Ad Blocking

Blocking ads and trackers for every device on the network at once, at the DNS level, rather than installing a browser extension on each device individually.

### Options
- **[[Pi-hole]]**: the long-standing default, simple web dashboard, huge community of blocklists.
- **[[AdGuard Home]]**: a newer alternative with a very similar approach, generally considered to have a slightly more modern UI and a few extra built-in features (like per-client rules and DNS-over-HTTPS/TLS upstream support out of the box), the two are close enough in practice that it mostly comes down to preference.
