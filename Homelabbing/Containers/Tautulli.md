# Tautulli

A monitoring and statistics dashboard specifically for **[[Plex]]**: who's watching what, from where, on what device, plus history and viewing habits over time. Can also send notifications (a new episode became available, a stream started buffering) and expose the data for use in a broader **[[Monitoring]]** setup like Grafana.

Purely Plex-specific, there isn't a direct equivalent bundled with Jellyfin, though Jellyfin exposes some of the same information through its own admin dashboard and plugins.
