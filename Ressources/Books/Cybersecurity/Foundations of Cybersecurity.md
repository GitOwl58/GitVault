# Foundations of Cybersecurity

**Author:** Jason Andress / **Edition:** 2026

An introduction to security fundamentals: CIA triad, authentication/authorization, threat modeling, risk management, crypto, and network/physical/operational security. 
A proper first course to learn how cybersecurity works, instead of handing you tools you can't use yet.
